# Coordinate Invariance

> Coordinates may change. Invariants must be preserved.
>
> 座標は変えてもよいが、不変量は保存する。

## Principle

Coordinates, representations, implementations, and runtime details are allowed to change. The invariants defined by this specification must be preserved across any such change.

## Scope

This principle applies to all layers of the Pi³XI model. A change of coordinates is acceptable only if the canonical invariants (see [`../invariants/phase1-invariants.md`](../invariants/phase1-invariants.md)) are preserved.
