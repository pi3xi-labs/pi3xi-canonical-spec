# Observation First

> Record observations, not explanations.
>
> 説明ではなく観測結果を記録する。

## Principle

The record captures what was observed. Explanations and interpretations are not part of the observation record; they belong to higher layers.

## Scope

This principle governs the Observe layer and the Canonical Record (see [`../runtime/canonical-record.md`](../runtime/canonical-record.md) and [`../runtime/observation-interface.md`](../runtime/observation-interface.md)).
